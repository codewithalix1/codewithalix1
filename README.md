## 👨‍💻 About Me

I’m a **Full-Stack Software Engineer and AI Engineer** with a **BS in Software Engineering from FAST-NUCES**. I enjoy turning ideas into reliable, user-friendly products—building everything from responsive frontends and backend services to APIs, databases, and production deployments.

My experience includes contributing to production web applications across **language learning, solar billing, real estate, and import-duty workflows**. At Softpers, I delivered product features, helped improve a language-learning app’s performance from **31% to 90%**, contributed to a platform serving **2,000+ active users**, and worked on AI-powered lesson generation and business workflow automation.

Alongside full-stack development, I’m exploring practical AI engineering with **LLMs, RAG, AI agents, and generative AI**, as well as automation with tools like **n8n**. I value clean, maintainable code, continuous learning, and shipping solutions that solve real problems.
